常見問題 FAQ

需要註冊帳戶嗎？ Do I need an account?

不需要。ShalomDays 不設帳戶或登入，你的資料儲存在你的裝置上。

No. ShalomDays has no accounts or sign-in. Your data is stored on your device.

訂閱包括甚麼？ What does the subscription include?

ShalomDays Pro 是自動續期的年費訂閱，可使用 App 全部功能。價格以 App 內，以及 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 顯示的當地價格為準。

ShalomDays Pro is an auto-renewing annual subscription that unlocks the full app. The price is shown in your local currency in the app and on the Apple App Store or Google Play.

7 日免費試用如何運作？ How does the 7-day free trial work?

首次合資格的訂閱者可享 7 日商店免費試用，試用期間可使用全部功能。是否合資格由 Apple 或 Google 決定。在 iOS，如你不在試用期結束前最少 24 小時取消，Apple 會自動按年費收費，之後每年自動續期。在 Android，如你不在試用期結束前取消，Google Play 會按年費收費，之後每年自動續期。

First-time eligible subscribers get a 7-day store trial, with full access during the trial. Eligibility is determined by Apple or Google. On iOS, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, Apple charges the annual price and the subscription renews every year. On Android, unless you cancel before the trial ends, Google Play charges the annual price and the subscription renews every year.

如何取消訂閱？ How do I cancel?

在 iPhone 或 iPad：設定 > [你的名字]（Apple ID）> 訂閱項目 > ShalomDays > 取消訂閱。取消後，你仍可使用至當期完結。刪除 App 並不會取消訂閱。

On iPhone or iPad: Settings > [your name] (Apple ID) > Subscriptions > ShalomDays > Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current period. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

你亦可以在此管理訂閱 · You can also manage it here: apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions

在 Android：請在 Google Play 的「付款與訂閱」管理或取消（Google Play 商店 > 個人資料圖示 > 付款與訂閱 > 訂閱 > ShalomDays > 取消訂閱）。刪除 App 並不會取消訂閱。

On Android: manage or cancel in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions (Google Play Store > profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > ShalomDays > Cancel subscription). Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

你亦可以在此管理 · You can also manage it here: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

如何恢復購買？ How do I restore my purchase?

在 iOS：如你更換裝置或重新安裝 App，請以購買時的同一個 Apple ID 登入 App Store，然後在 App 的訂閱頁面按「恢復購買」。

On iOS: if you change devices or reinstall, sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe, then tap “Restore Purchases” on the subscription screen.

在 Android：請使用訂閱時的同一個 Google 帳戶恢復購買。

On Android: restore with the same Google account you used to subscribe.

如何申請退款？ How do I request a refund?

在 iOS，所有付款由 Apple 處理，我們無法直接退款。請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 以你的 Apple ID 登入並申請退款。

On iOS, all payments are processed by Apple, so we can’t issue refunds ourselves. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and request a refund.

在 Android，退款由 Google 處理，並非由開發者處理。請使用 Google Play 的退款或「要求退款」流程：support.google.com/googleplay/answer/15574897。

On Android, refunds are handled by Google, not the developer. Use Google Play’s refund or “request a refund” flow: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/15574897.

如何刪除我的資料？ How do I delete my data?

ShalomDays 沒有帳戶，我們亦沒有伺服器儲存你的資料。刪除 App 即會刪除 App 在裝置上的資料（行程、設定、自訂背景）。已同步到裝置日曆的行程會保留在裝置日曆內，可在「日曆」App 中刪除。

ShalomDays has no accounts, and we don’t store your data on any server. Deleting the app removes the data it stores on the device (events, settings, custom background). Events you synced to your device calendar stay there; you can delete them in the Calendar app.

在 Android，由於已啟用自動備份，系統可能把裝置上的 App 資料（包括首次開啟時間、設定及行程）備份到你自己的 Google 帳戶，以便重新安裝後還原。我們無法存取該備份，這並非由我們收集你的個人資料。

On Android, because Auto Backup is enabled, the system may back up on-device app data (including a first-open timestamp, settings and events) to your own Google account so it can be restored after reinstall. We cannot access that backup. This is not us collecting your personal data.

沒有提醒通知？ Not getting reminders?

在 iOS，請到 設定 > 通知 > ShalomDays 確認已允許通知。在 Android，請到 設定 > 應用程式 > ShalomDays > 通知 確認已允許通知。

On iOS, check Settings > Notifications > ShalomDays and make sure notifications are allowed. On Android, check Settings > Apps > ShalomDays > Notifications and make sure notifications are allowed.

日曆同步不到？ Calendar sync not working?

在 iOS，請到 設定 > 私隱與保安 > 日曆 > ShalomDays，選擇「完整取用權」。在 Android，請到 設定 > 應用程式 > ShalomDays > 權限，允許日曆。

On iOS, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars > ShalomDays and choose “Full Access”. On Android, go to Settings > Apps > ShalomDays > Permissions and allow Calendar.

假期或節期資料沒有更新？ Holiday data not updating?

App 可離線使用內置資料，連線時會在背景更新。在中國內地如未能更新，請稍後再試。

The app works offline with built-in data and updates in the background when you’re online. If updates fail in mainland China, please try again later.
