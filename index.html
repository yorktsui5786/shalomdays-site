ShalomDays 猶太日曆

A Jewish calendar for Hong Kong, mainland China and overseas users.

ShalomDays 將希伯來曆日期、猶太節期及各地公眾假期整合在一個簡潔的日曆內，並支援繁體中文、簡體中文及英文。

ShalomDays brings Hebrew dates, Jewish holidays and festivals, and public holidays together in one clean calendar, in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English.

功能 Features

  • 希伯來曆日期及猶太節期介紹 — Hebrew dates and Jewish holiday details
  • 香港、澳門、台灣、內地及其他地區公眾假期 — Public holidays for Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, mainland China and other regions
  • 自訂行程及本機提醒 — Personal events with local reminders
  • 可選擇與裝置日曆同步 — Optional sync with your device calendars
  • 主畫面小工具、自訂背景及深色模式 — Home Screen widget, custom backgrounds and dark mode
  • 可離線使用 — Works offline

訂閱 Subscription

ShalomDays Pro 年費訂閱包括 7 日免費試用，經 Apple App Store 收費及管理。

ShalomDays Pro is an annual subscription with a 7-day free trial, billed and managed by Apple through the App Store.
